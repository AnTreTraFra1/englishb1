Plan Your Visit

Everything you need to know to visit the Archaeological Park of Velia: opening hours, tickets, directions, and useful tips.

📍

Location

Archaeological Park of Velia
Piazzale Amedeo Maiuri
84046 Ascea Marina (SA)
Campania, Italy

🕐

Opening Hours

Open every day:
8:30 – 19:30

Last ticket: 18:30
Last full visit: 17:30

The park is open all year, including holidays.

🎫

Tickets

Full price: Check the official site for current prices.

Free admission:
Every first Sunday of the month
25 April, 2 June, 4 November

Valid for 3 days — includes Paestum & Velia.

🚗

How to Get There

By car: Take the A2 motorway, exit "Ascea". Follow the signs for the Archaeological Park of Velia.

By train: Ascea Marina station is 2 km from the park. You can walk or take a taxi.

By bus: Regular buses from Salerno to Ascea Marina.

Useful Tips

• Wear comfortable shoes — the terrain is uneven.
• Bring water, especially in summer.
• The visit takes about 2–3 hours.
• You can buy tickets online through the "Musei Italiani" app.
• Booking is not mandatory.

Plan Your Route

Velia is located in the Cilento area, about 40 km from Paestum and about 100 km from Salerno. You can easily visit both Paestum and Velia in one day or over a weekend.

The ticket for the Archaeological Parks of Paestum and Velia is valid for 3 consecutive days. This means you can visit Paestum one day and Velia the next, or visit both on the same day if you have enough time.

The park is part of the Cilento, Vallo di Diano and Alburni National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site. The area is famous for its beautiful nature, beaches, and traditional food.
