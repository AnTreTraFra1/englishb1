The Eleatic School

"What is, is; what is not, is not." — Parmenides. Discover the philosophers who changed Western thought.

Parmenides of Elea

Parmenides was born in Elea around 515 BC. He is one of the most important philosophers of ancient Greece. His main idea is: "What is, is; what is not, is not." In other words, being exists and non-being does not exist.

He believed that change is an illusion. According to Parmenides, reality is one, eternal, and unchanging. Our senses show us a world of change and movement, but this is only appearance. True reality can only be understood through reason and logic.

His poem "On Nature" explains these ideas. Only fragments of this poem survive today, but they have influenced philosophy for over 2,500 years.

Zeno of Elea

Zeno was a student and friend of Parmenides. He was born in Elea around 490 BC. He is famous for his paradoxes — logical arguments that seem impossible but are difficult to solve.

The most famous paradox is "Achilles and the Tortoise". In this story, the hero Achilles races against a tortoise. The tortoise starts with a small advantage. Zeno argues that Achilles can never catch the tortoise: every time Achilles reaches the tortoise's position, the tortoise has moved a little forward. This creates an infinite series of steps.

Zeno also argued that an arrow cannot move, because at each moment of time it is in a fixed position. These paradoxes are still discussed today in mathematics, physics, and philosophy.

The Legacy of the Eleatic School

The Eleatic School had a big influence on Western philosophy. Plato and Aristotle discussed the ideas of Parmenides and Zeno in their own works. The question "is reality one or many?" is still a central question in philosophy.

Zeno's paradoxes are important in modern mathematics, especially in the study of infinity and limits. The concept of the "infinite series" in calculus is connected to Zeno's work.

The discovery of the helmets of Velia in 2026 also connects to philosophy: archaeologists and historians believe that the strong intellectual tradition of Elea was rooted in its identity as a community of survivors who valued wisdom as much as strength.

Key Ideas in Simple Words

✨

Reality is One

The world is not made of many different things. Deep down, everything is one single, unchanging reality.

❓

Change is an Illusion

What we see as change is only appearance. True reality never changes. Our senses can be wrong.

🔢

Infinity is Real

Zeno's paradoxes show that space, time, and motion can be divided into infinite parts. This idea is still used in modern physics.
